## Intention Prompts (30 total — 5 per theme)

These ask "what will you do today?" — shown as the first journal prompt each morning.

### Finding calm
1. What's one thing I can let go of today?
2. What would make today feel manageable, even if not perfect?
3. Where can I build in a moment of stillness today?
4. What's the one thing that actually needs my attention today?
5. What boundary can I set today to protect my peace?

### Processing loss
6. What's one gentle thing I can do for myself today?
7. How can I honor what I'm feeling without fighting it?
8. Who or what can I lean on today?
9. What small comfort can I give myself permission to enjoy today?
10. What would the person I'm missing want for me today?

### Building gratitude
11. What ordinary moment am I going to pay closer attention to today?
12. Who deserves a thank you I haven't given yet?
13. What simple pleasure am I going to savor today?
14. How can I bring a little more joy into someone else's day?
15. What's something I usually rush through that I'll slow down for today?

### Seeking growth
16. What's one important thing I want to accomplish today?
17. What would make me proud of myself at the end of today?
18. What's something worth doing today, even if it takes courage?
19. What's one small step I can take toward something I care about?
20. What habit am I trying to build, and how can I practice it today?

### Strengthening connections
21. Who can I help, connect with, or positively impact today?
22. Who do I care about, and what could I do for them today?
23. How can I be more present with the people I see today?
24. Who might need to hear from me today?
25. What's one way I can be a better listener today?

### General
26. What can I do today that I'll feel good about tonight?
27. What does a good day look like for me today?
28. What's one thing I'm going to do differently today?
29. What matters most to me today?
30. If I could only do one thing today, what would it be?

## Gratitude Prompts (30 total — 5 per theme)

These ask "what are you grateful for?" — shown as the second journal prompt each morning.

### Finding calm
1. What's one source of peace in my life right now?
2. What helped me get through a hard moment recently?
3. What part of my routine brings me the most comfort?
4. What's a place that always makes me feel safe?
5. What sound or smell instantly calms me?

### Processing loss
6. What's a quality I received from someone I miss?
7. What's still here that I'm grateful for, even in this season?
8. What moment of kindness has stayed with me?
9. What memory still makes me smile, even through the sadness?
10. Who has held space for me when I needed it most?

### Building gratitude
11. What am I feeling grateful for today?
12. What's going right that I haven't stopped to acknowledge?
13. What's one small, ordinary thing I'm glad exists?
14. What am I walking past every day without really noticing?
15. What's something I take for granted that someone else would love to have?

### Seeking growth
16. What challenge taught me something I'm now grateful for?
17. What's a strength I have that I don't give myself enough credit for?
18. What's something I can do now that I couldn't a year ago?
19. What difficult decision am I glad I made?
20. What's a habit I've built that I'm proud of?

### Strengthening connections
21. Who made my life a little easier recently?
22. What relationship am I most grateful for right now?
23. When did I last feel truly seen by someone?
24. What's a conversation that changed how I see things?
25. Who is someone that believes in me?

### General
26. What would I miss if it were gone tomorrow?
27. What am I looking forward to?
28. What happened recently that I want to remember?
29. Who made my life a little easier recently without me saying thank you?
30. What's the best thing that happened this week?
